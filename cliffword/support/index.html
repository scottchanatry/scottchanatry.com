Common questions

Why does Cliffword download 1.1 GB after I install it?

That is the language model that scores your answers and writes the story. It runs on your phone, so it has to live there. The download happens once; you can keep using the app while it finishes. Wi-Fi is best.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the model is downloaded. On-device speech recognition may need your phone's Spanish speech files; the app tells you if they are missing.

Do I need Apple Music?

No. You can paste lyrics for any song and practise them by hand. With an Apple Music subscription on iPhone, the song plays and pauses at each line for you.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your phone. In the app: Profile, Settings, Reset progress (clears your progress and history) or Delete model. Deleting the app removes everything.

A story sentence was wrong or offensive.

Use "Report this sentence" in the app, or write to hello@scottchanatry.com with the sentence. Stories are written by a small language model on your phone, so mistakes can happen.
