Speak Spanish by finishing the sentence.

A story stops mid-sentence and you say what comes next, out loud. Any natural answer counts.

How it works

  • The story is told out loud, one sentence at a time, from words you know plus one or two new ones.
  • When it stops, hold anywhere and say what comes next. A small language model on your phone scores how natural your answer is.
  • Your own sentences are shaded where they surprise a Spanish speaker, and you get a chance to fix them before you see the answer.
  • Bring songs: paste lyrics, sing the line back, and learn the slang of Puerto Rico, the Dominican Republic, Cuba and Colombia.
  • The headline number is minutes spoken.

Private by design

The language model and speech recognition run on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Read the privacy policy.
