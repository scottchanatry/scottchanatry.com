Sound Garden

November 15, 2026

I have a new app out, and I’m excited about this one. Sound Garden is a small generative music app for iOS and Android. You tap to plant dots, the dots ring like bells, and the garden keeps playing by itself.

How it plays

  • Tap anywhere to plant a dot. Up to fourteen fit.
  • Height is pitch. Higher on the screen is a higher bell. The ten notes are a pentatonic scale, so any combination sounds good together.
  • Left to right is tempo. Each dot pulses by itself every few seconds, and dots further right pulse more slowly.
  • Ripples carry sound. When a ripple reaches another dot, that dot answers with a quieter echo, so the space between dots becomes the rhythm.
  • Tap a dot to ring it. Hold a dot to pull it up.

There is nothing to win and nothing to finish. Plant a few dots, set the phone down, and listen to what they work out between them.

How it is built

Sound Garden is one codebase in React Native with Expo, so the same code runs on iPhone and Android. The bell tones and the app icon are generated by short scripts in the repository, and the whole thing is open source under the MIT license.

Try it

It isn’t in the App Store or Google Play yet. For now you can run it from the source: install the free Expo Go app on your phone, follow the two commands in the README, and scan the code it shows you.
