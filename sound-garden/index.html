Sound Garden

A slow, generative instrument. Tap to plant seeds. Each seed grows into a plant that rings a bell note and sends out a ripple, and the plants the ripple reaches answer with a quieter echo.

How it plays

  • Higher on the screen is a higher note. Further right is a slower beat.
  • Tap a plant to ring it. Hold a plant to pull it up.
  • Scatter sows a handful of seeds. Pause lets the garden rest.

More about how it was made: the Sound Garden post. The source code is on GitHub.
