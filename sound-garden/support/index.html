Common questions

I can't hear anything.

Turn the volume up. On iPhone, also check that the ringer switch is not on silent, and that the phone is not connected to a speaker or headphones you can't hear.

Nothing happens when I tap.

Seeds can only be planted in the open ground between the title and the buttons. A tap close to a plant rings that plant instead of planting a new one. The garden holds 14 plants; when it is full, hold a plant to pull it up.

The garden went quiet.

If the round button shows a play symbol, the garden is resting. Tap it to wake the garden.

I cleared the garden by mistake.

For five seconds after Clear, the button reads Undo. Tap it to put the plants back.

Does it save my garden?

No. Each garden lasts until you close the app.

Does it work with VoiceOver and TalkBack?

Yes. The planting bed offers actions to plant a low, middle or high note, and each plant can be rung or pulled up. The app also calms its animation when Reduce Motion is on.
