Sound Garden Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Sound Garden is a music app for iPhone and Android, made by Scott Chanatry, an individual developer ("I" and "me" below). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Sound Garden collects no information about you.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.
  • It does not connect to the internet. Its sounds, drawings and typeface are all inside the app.
  • It asks for no permissions: no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.
  • It does not save your garden. When you close the app, the garden is gone.

What Apple and Google may share with me

Apple and Google may show me download numbers and other statistics without names. If you choose, in your phone's settings, to share crash reports or diagnostics with app developers, they may give me crash reports for Sound Garden (for example, the phone model, the system version and where the app stopped). These come from Apple and Google under their own terms. The app itself sends nothing.

If you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use them only to answer you, and I delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Children

Sound Garden is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to look up, correct, export or delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Selling and sharing

I do not sell or share personal information, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Sound Garden ever starts collecting any information, this policy and the app's store privacy labels will say so before the version that does it is released.
