About

I’m Scott Chanatry. Much of my working life was spent doing the slowest, most valuable thing there is: listening to kids, and to the families around them, until the picture makes sense. For now I’ve stepped away to learn what training couldn’t teach me — by being home with my own children while they’re young.

Writing fiction and coding keep me busy the rest of the time. There are two books so far, Behold Your Mother and Nobody Sent Me, with a parenting blog on the way. The coding is small software for the fun of it — a habit that started at Stanford and never really stopped.
