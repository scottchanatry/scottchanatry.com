About

I’m Scott Chanatry, a child psychiatrist. Much of my working life was spent doing the slowest, most valuable thing there is: listening to kids, and to the families around them, until the picture makes sense. For now I’ve stepped away from clinical practice to learn what training couldn’t teach me — by being home with my own children while they’re young.

Whatever’s left over goes to building small software for the fun of it — a habit that started at Stanford and never really stopped.
